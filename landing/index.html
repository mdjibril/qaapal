NSQAssessment is a dedicated writing portal designed specifically for National Skills Qualification (NSQ) assessors. It takes raw observation summaries or session logs, structures them into detailed technical statements, and maps them to NSQ Performance Criteria (PC) and Learning Outcomes (LO) for standard-compliant assessment reports.

The BYOK model allows you to pay a low monthly fee for our platform software while entering your own personal AI API key (Google Gemini, Groq, or OpenRouter). You are then billed directly by the AI provider at wholesale rate. Because AI costs are extremely low (fractions of a cent per page), this saves you significant money over standard flat-rate SaaS platforms that resell AI credits at massive markups.

Getting a key is free and takes under 3 minutes. For Google Gemini, visit Google AI Studio. For Groq, register on the Groq Console. Simply generate a key, copy it, and paste it into your NSQAssessment account settings. The settings are secured using state-of-the-art encryption.
