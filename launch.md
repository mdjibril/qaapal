# 🚀 NSQ Assessment Portal Launch Strategy

This document outlines the high-conversion launch sequence for `nsqassessment.com.ng`, specifically designed for professional WhatsApp groups (Assessors, IVs, and EQAs).

## 📹 Asset 1: The "Speedrun" Video
**Goal:** Show immediate value in under 90 seconds.
**Recording Instructions:**
1. **Start at the Dashboard:** Show a screen recording of you selecting a Trade (e.g., ICT).
2. **The Magic Selection:** Quickly check 5-10 Performance Criteria across 2 different Units.
3. **The "Human" Input:** Type a short, messy observation note: *"Student struggled with crimping at first, but successfully completed the network cable test on second attempt."*
4. **The Synthesis:** Click "Generate." Let the viewer see the "AI is crafting..." status.
5. **The Reveal:** Scroll through the generated narrative. **Zoom in** on an inline mapping like `(ICT/SMC/004/L2 - LO1:PC 1.2)`.
6. **The Export:** Click "Download Word" and open the document to show the professional `CPN-ARF-02` layout.

## 🛠️ Technical Checklist for Launch

1. **Deep Link Verification:** Ensure that clicking the link with `?intent=earlybird` correctly opens the "Sign Up" tab on your site.
2. **Mobile Optimization:** Most WhatsApp users will click from their phones. Ensure the sidebar is easy to toggle and the generation process works smoothly on mobile browsers.
3. **Credit Monitoring:** Check your Supabase dashboard regularly to see who has hit 0 credits so you can manually upgrade the "First 100" in the `organizations` table.
4. **Feedback Loop:** Add a small "Support/Feedback" WhatsApp link or email in the sidebar so users can report issues during the first week.

## 💡 Marketing Hooks to Remember
- **"Audit-Ready Paperwork":** Use this phrase. EQAs and IVs love it.
- **"Compliance First":** Remind them that the PC codes are pulled from the official database, reducing mapping errors.
- **"The End of 2 AM Reports":** Emphasize the time-saving aspect.
